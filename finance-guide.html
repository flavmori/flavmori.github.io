4. Decision Engine

Priority-ordered actions with explanations:

  1. Rebuild Liquidity — if invested > target AND liquid < target. Two options: stop investing (free) or sell (tax cost shown).
  2. Rebalance Equity/Bond — if ratio drifts >5pts. Two options: future tranches 100% into underweight (free) or sell/buy (tax cost shown).
  3. Move Cash to Money Market — if surplus exists and MM below sub-target.
  4. Invest Surplus — excess liquidity into Equity+Bond, capped. Gap-weighted split. Dynamic cap x1.5 when market is down (gain% negative).
  5. Accumulate — if everything is below target, wait for income.
  6. MM/Cash Rebalance — at regime, fix 60/40 split within liquid.
  7. Satellite (BTC) — buy in 3 tranches at target prices (€42K/€35K/€28K). Sell in 3 tranches (€80K/€95K/€110K, 33/33/34% split).

6. Projection

Interactive compound growth chart. Hover on the chart to see exact values at any point in time.

Controls:

  • Scenario buttons: Pessimistic (4%/1%) · Base (8%/2.5%) · Optimistic (12%/3.5%)
  • Time horizon: 5Y · 10Y · 15Y · 20Y · 30Y
  • Scope: "Investments Only" (ACWI+Bond) or "Full Portfolio" (includes MM at 2%, Cash at 0%, BTC flat)
  • Inflation: "Nominal" or "Real (-2% inflation)" — subtracts 2% from all returns

Stacked layers:

  • Grey = your contributions (money you put in)
  • Medium color = returns (market growth on your money)
  • Light color = compound (returns on returns — the snowball effect)

Growth breakdown: shows how much each instrument contributed (Equity, Bond, MM).

8. I Need Cash

Priority order for withdrawals:

  1. Cash above floor (free, instant)
  2. Cash emergency reserve (touches floor)
  3. Money Market (1-2 days, taxed as capital gain)
  4. Bond (1-2 days, taxed)
  5. Equity (1-2 days, taxed, avoid if possible)
  6. Satellite/BTC (1-3 days, highest tax)

9. Settings Reference

  • Investment Cap: max per invest action (default €2,500)
  • Monthly Contribution: estimated monthly investment for projection (default €400)
  • Unrealized gain %: read from TR "Since buy" — used for tax estimates and dynamic cap
  • BTC Tranches: buy levels (€42K/€35K/€28K) and sell levels (€80K/€95K/€110K)
  • GitHub Token: for cross-device sync (gist scope)

10. How to Use

  • Open every 1-2 weeks
  • Update balances from Trade Republic
  • Update gain% from TR "Since buy"
  • Follow the numbered Actions
  • Click Save
  • Use projection to motivate yourself (switch time horizons, toggle BTC)
