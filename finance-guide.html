Portfolio Rebalancer

How the tool works — logic, formulas, and decision engine

1. Structure

Your portfolio is divided into 3 top-level buckets, each with sub-allocations:

BucketDefault %Sub-allocation
Invested80%Equity 80% / Bond 20%
Liquid15%Money Market 60% / Cash 40%
Satellite5%BTC (or other high-risk)

All percentages are configurable in Settings. Top-level must sum to 100%. Sub-allocations must also sum to 100% within their bucket.

2. How Targets Are Calculated

Equity target = Total × Invested% × Equity ratio%
Bond target = Total × Invested% × Bond ratio%
Money Market target = Total × Liquid% × MM ratio%
Cash target = Total × Liquid% × Cash ratio%
Satellite target = Total × Satellite%

Example with €50,000 and defaults:

AssetCalculationTarget €
Equity (ACWI)50K × 80% × 80%€32,000
Bond (Gov EUR)50K × 80% × 20%€8,000
Money Market (CSH2)50K × 15% × 60%€4,500
Cash (Accounts)50K × 15% × 40%€3,000
Satellite (BTC)50K × 5%€2,500

3. The Decision Engine

Every time you update balances, the tool evaluates these conditions in order:

Step 1: Sell Invested → Rebuild Liquidity

Triggers when: Invested is over target by >€500 AND Liquid is under target by >€500

What it means: Markets went up, your portfolio drifted too heavy into investments. You need to take profit and rebuild your safety net.

Amount: min(invested excess, liquid deficit)

Step 2: Rebalance Equity/Bond

Triggers when: The ratio between Equity and Bond drifts more than 5 percentage points from target

What it means: One asset grew faster than the other. Sell the winner, buy the loser to maintain your risk profile.

Amount: difference between actual equity and ideal equity within invested

Step 3: Deploy Surplus (when Liquid > Target)

If you have more liquidity than needed:

  • 3a. Fill Money Market — if MM is below its sub-target, move cash there first (earns yield)
  • 3b. Invest — remaining surplus goes into Equity + Bond, split by their gaps. Capped per tranche.

Cap: You set a maximum per investment (default €2,500). This enforces DCA — you never invest everything at once.

Step 4: Accumulate

Triggers when: Liquid is below target AND Invested is also below target

What it means: You don't have enough anywhere. Wait for income before investing.

Step 5: MM/Cash Rebalance (Regime)

Triggers when: Liquid is approximately at target (±€500) but the MM/Cash ratio inside is off by >€200

What it means: You're at steady state but the split between remunerated and non-remunerated cash is wrong. Move between them.

Step 6: Satellite

  • Below target (by >€100): "Buy BTC at your entry price" — capped separately
  • Above target (by >€100): "Consider taking profit at your sell price"

4. Caps

CapApplies toPurpose
Investment CapEquity + BondDCA — don't invest all surplus at once
Satellite CapBTCControl high-risk entry size

Money Market moves have no cap — it's practically cash, zero risk to enter fully.

5. Investment Split Logic

When investing, the split between Equity and Bond is gap-weighted, not fixed:

Equity amount = Investment × (Equity gap / Total gap)
Bond amount = Investment − Equity amount

If Equity is more below target than Bond, more goes to Equity. This naturally rebalances toward 80/20 with every tranche.

6. When to Use the Tool

  • Every 2 weeks (bulk phase): update balances, follow the suggested action, invest the cap amount
  • Monthly (regime): update balances after salary, follow suggestions
  • After market moves: check if rebalancing triggered
  • After large expenses: check if liquidity dropped below target

7. Cloud Sync

Your data syncs to a private GitHub Gist. To enable:

  • Go to Settings → paste your GitHub token (needs "gist" scope)
  • Every time you click Save, it syncs automatically
  • Opening the tool on a new device loads from cloud automatically
Important: The tool never invests for you. It only suggests actions. You execute them manually on Trade Republic. The suggestions are based on math — they don't consider market sentiment, news, or timing. That's by design: systematic investing outperforms emotional decisions.
