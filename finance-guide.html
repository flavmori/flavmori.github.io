2. Target Calculation

Equity target = Total x Invested% x Equity ratio%
Bond target = Total x Invested% x Bond ratio%
Money Market target = Total x Liquid% x MM ratio%
Cash target = Total x Liquid% x Cash ratio%
Satellite target = Total x Satellite%

3. Decision Engine (Actions)

When you update balances, the tool evaluates in priority order:

1. Rebuild Liquidity

When: Invested > target + €500 AND Liquid < target - €500

What: Markets rallied, portfolio drifted too heavy. Shows two options: (A) stop investing and let salary rebuild liquid (free), or (B) sell investments (tax cost shown).

2. Rebalance Equity/Bond

When: Equity/Bond ratio drifts more than 5 percentage points from target

What: Shows two options: (A) invest next N tranches 100% into the underweight asset (free), or (B) sell overweight and buy underweight (tax cost shown).

3. Move Cash to Money Market

When: Liquid > target (surplus exists) AND Money Market is below its sub-target

What: Fill MM before investing — it earns yield while you wait.

4. Invest Surplus

When: Liquid > target by more than €300

What: Invest the surplus, capped at Investment Cap per session. Split between Equity and Bond based on which is more below target (gap-weighted, not fixed 80/20).

Dynamic cap: If your equity gain% is negative (market is down), the cap increases by 1.5x — you invest more when it's cheaper.

5. Accumulate

When: Liquid below target AND Invested also below target

What: Don't invest. Wait for income to rebuild liquidity first.

6. MM/Cash Rebalance

When: Liquid is approximately at target (within €500) but MM/Cash split is off by more than €200

What: Move between MM and Cash to maintain the 60/40 ratio within liquid.

7. Satellite (BTC)

Buy: When satellite is below target by more than €100. Budget split into 3 equal tranches at price levels you set (default: €50K, €45K, €38K). Buy each tranche when BTC reaches that level.

Sell: When satellite is over target by more than €100. Sell in 3 tranches at price levels you set (default: €80K, €95K, €110K). 33/33/34% split.

4. Investment Cap

The cap limits how much you invest per session (default €2,500). This enforces DCA — you never deploy all surplus at once. The tool calculates how many tranches remain.

When equity gain% is negative: cap x 1.5 (buy more when market is cheaper).

5. Tax Awareness

When selling is suggested, the tool estimates tax cost using:

Tax = Amount sold x (gain% / (100 + gain%)) x tax rate (19-21%)

You set your unrealized gain% per asset in Settings (read from Trade Republic "Since buy" view).

6. I Need Cash

Enter an amount and the tool shows where to take it from, in priority order:

  1. Cash above floor (free, instant)
  2. Cash emergency reserve (touches floor, rebuild after)
  3. Money Market (1-2 days, no tax)
  4. Bond (1-2 days, low tax)
  5. Equity (1-2 days, medium tax — avoid if possible)
  6. Satellite/BTC (1-3 days, highest tax)

7. Cloud Sync

Settings > GitHub Token. Save once, syncs automatically. Opening on a new device loads from cloud if token is present.

8. How to Use

  • Open every 1-2 weeks (or monthly)
  • Update balances from Trade Republic
  • Update gain% from Trade Republic "Since buy"
  • Follow the numbered Actions
  • Click Save when done
The tool suggests. You execute. It does not connect to your broker or make trades. It uses math, not market sentiment. Systematic investing outperforms emotional decisions.
